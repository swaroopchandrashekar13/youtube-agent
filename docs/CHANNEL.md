# Channel portfolio research

Status: **options for the owner to choose from.** Researched 2026-11-15. Supersedes the earlier "Kannada tech careers" recommendation, which the owner declined.

Most RPM and competition numbers below come from vendor or SEO blogs with no published method, and they often disagree. Use them to rank ideas, not to forecast income. Real numbers come from YouTube Studio once a channel exists.

## What the research says about the rules of the game (2026)

1. **Originality decides monetisation, not faces or AI.** YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy (renamed July 2025, reportedly clarified July 2026) targets *mass-produced, templated, repetitive* channels. AI voice and faceless formats stay eligible when each video has original scripting, analysis and editing. Enforcement is **channel-level**. → Every channel needs a format where each video is naturally different (new data, a new story, a new case). Our Phase 7 sameness check matters.
2. **Kids content is off the table.** There is strong 2026 pressure on AI content for kids, YouTube Kids limits AI to vetted channels, and labelling rules are tightening.
3. **Advice personas are off the table.** No AI personas giving finance, medical or legal advice (YouTube policy and our CLAUDE.md rule 7). Educational explainers with sources are fine; "what you should invest in" is not.
4. **One channel can serve many languages.** Multi-language audio tracks are available to all creators (Sept 2025), and YouTube says it's easier than running separate language channels. Creators using them got **25%+ of watch time from non-primary languages**. → Instead of one channel per language, **one channel per subject, with Hindi/Kannada/Tamil/Telugu audio tracks** produced by our TTS layer. (Whether the Data API can upload audio tracks, or only Studio can, must be verified in Phase 8.)
5. **Multiple channels share risk.** Guides disagree on AdSense details, but one person normally has one AdSense account, so a policy problem on one channel can affect the others. Each Google Cloud project's API quota is also shared across the channels it serves. → Start with **two** channels and add more once one is monetised and clean.
6. **24/7 looped live streams are risky.** The monetisation policy explicitly covers live streams. Looping isn't banned, but "low-value repetitive programming" can be demonetised even if it stays online. Live works best when it is genuinely live: real-time data, scheduled events, Q&A.

## Shortlist

Scored for fit with our pipeline: code-rendered visuals (Revideo/Playwright), TTS narration, cited sources, no GPU, low cost, and policy safety.

Animated charts and maps explaining one dataset per video (city growth, monsoon rainfall, railways, cricket history, elections history, prices) | Data-viz is repeatedly named as an under-used faceless niche with an educated, higher-RPM audience. **Not recommended:** kids/cartoons (point 2), personal finance tips (point 3), generic news (accuracy and legal risk), "top 10" compilations and reused clips (reused-content policy), AI lofi music dumps (Content ID plus repetition).

## Recommended portfolio

1. **Start now: Channel A (Data stories) + Channel B (Scam & cyber-safety).** Both make every video naturally unique, both have strong source material, and both reuse the same pipeline: chart/map templates for A, explainer and "red flags" templates for B. English or Hinglish as the main track, with Hindi + Kannada audio tracks (others later).
2. **Live, phase 1:** **scheduled** live streams rendered by our job on demand: a weekly scam round-up, and data dashboards during real events (smog season, monsoon, cricket, elections). This keeps the "no always-on" cost rule intact.
3. **Live, phase 2 (optional, separate approval):** one always-on data dashboard (for example live AQI) on a small VPS. Vendor pages put the cheapest options at about **€2–8/month** (verify bandwidth caps: a 1080p stream moves ~3 TB/month). This needs an explicit exception to CLAUDE.md rule 6 or a non-AWS host.
4. **After one channel is monetised:** add **C (history on maps)** or **D (space)**. Keep E and F as experiments.

## What changes in the build plan

| Phase | Change |
|---|---|
| 3-4 | Multi-channel config (`channels/<id>.yaml`: niche, languages, templates, voice, schedule). TTS produces a **main track plus per-language dub tracks** |
| 5 | Briefs carry `channelId`; strategy prompts are per channel |
| 6 | Template families per channel: charts/maps (A, C, D), scam-flow diagrams (B) |
| 8 | Verify the API for multi-language audio tracks; per-project quota budget shared across channels |
| New | **Live stage**: render a dashboard or scene with Playwright/Revideo → ffmpeg → RTMP to a YouTube `liveBroadcast`, started on schedule or approval and stopped after N hours. Human approval applies to the stream's setup (title, data sources, duration), not every frame |

## Decisions for the owner

1. Which two channels to start with (recommended: A + B).
2. Main language per channel (English, Hinglish, Hindi, Kannada) and which dub tracks to add.
3. Whether to allow one always-on live dashboard later (a small monthly cost outside the burst-job model).
